# Proposal QA pipeline

<!-- New in this derivative; see ../../../NOTICE. -->

Select an acceptance bar appropriate to the artifact: submission, proposal,
internal draft, or quick triage. Do not apply a numeric threshold without first
checking hard evidence blockers.

## Order

1. **Scientific content:** check claim support, feasibility, novelty framing,
   methods, scope, and missing controls with only the relevant specialists.
2. **Evidence and structure:** validate claim-to-source mapping, section
   contracts, internal consistency, numbering, and reproducibility.
3. **Language:** remove inflated, generic, or template-like phrasing without
   strengthening claims.
4. **Rubric:** score with `evaluation-rubric.md`, record dimension-level evidence,
   and direct revision to the weakest consequential dimension.

Run at most three corrective rounds unless the user requests another budget.
Stop earlier when quality plateaus, specialists expose irreconcilable constraints,
or missing evidence prevents a defensible revision.

## Deliverable

Return:

- artifact and QA mode;
- hard blockers;
- dimension scores with evidence;
- accepted changes and deferred changes;
- a narrow revision brief;
- stop/continue recommendation.
