# Bibliography anomaly patterns

These are signals to investigate, never automatic correction rules. Determine
the cited manifestation, target style, and controlling authoritative record
before suggesting a value.

## Dates and versions

- DOI strings, submission dates, online dates, conference dates, proceedings
  dates, and issue/volume years may differ.
- Identify whether the citation targets the preprint, conference paper,
  online-first article, version of record, or later issue assignment.
- Do not derive a citation year from digits in a DOI or mechanically prefer
  either the online or volume year.

## Authors

- Verify identity and author order against the authoritative record.
- Accents, transliteration, initials, surname particles, compound surnames, and
  group authors can have multiple representations, but reordering or dropping a
  surname can change identity.
- Normalize presentation only when the target style permits it; preserve the
  verified identity and sequence.

## Pages, article numbers, and venues

- Preprints, proceedings, journal versions, editions, and article-number
  journals can legitimately differ.
- Respect the historical venue/publisher name present on the cited
  manifestation. Do not modernize it by assumption.
- For conferences, distinguish event date from proceedings publication date
  and follow the selected manifestation and target style.

## DOI and registry results

- A DOI that resolves to a different title/author set is a critical mismatch
  candidate, not an automatic replacement.
- Registry `not_found` means only that the queried registry did not return a
  record. It does not prove invalidity or identify another registrar.
- Preprints and versions of record may have different identifiers. Do not
  always replace one with the other; first establish which work the author
  intends to cite.

## Non-journal sources

For theses, standards, software, products, reports, and manuals, verify the
issuing institution, edition/version, stable identifier or URL, and release
date. Apply the requested citation style only after identity is established.
